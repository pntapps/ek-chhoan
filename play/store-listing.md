## English (en-US)

**App name (29/30)**

```
Ek Chhoan: Simple Home Screen
```

**Short description (77/80)**
```
A simple home screen for elders: big family photos to call, four big buttons.
```

**Full description**
```
Ek Chhoan (এক ছোঁয়া, "one touch") is a simple home screen for elders. It replaces a busy phone screen with one calm page that can be used without reading or typing.

WHAT IS ON THE SCREEN
• A greeting and today's date, in English, Bengali or Hindi
• Up to 6 family cards with a big photo and name. Touch a card to call that person.
• Four big buttons: YouTube, Camera, Gallery and Torch
• A large internet status bar that shows whether Wi-Fi or mobile data is working. Touch it to open the phone's internet settings.
• A small settings button for the family

MADE FOR OLDER EYES AND HANDS
• Very large buttons and text, with strong contrast
• Three text sizes, and it follows the phone's font size
• Every touch gives a short vibration
• Optional voice readout that speaks each button aloud (uses the phone's text-to-speech, in the app's language when that voice is installed)
• English, Bengali (বাংলা) and Hindi (हिन्दी)
• Six colour themes: Classic, Classic Dark, Sunrise, Ocean, Meadow and Midnight
• Icons with names or icons only, in normal, large or extra-large size
• Nothing to swipe and no small menus. Back does nothing on the home screen, so you can't get lost.

SET UP BY FAMILY IN MINUTES
• Add family members with a name, phone number and photo, and make extra groups such as Friends or Office. You can pick the number from contacts and the photo from the gallery.
• Choose which buttons to show, and add up to 4 favourite apps (such as WhatsApp) as big buttons
• Settings are kept behind a simple 3-picture lock, so the screen stays the way you set it up
• Switch back to the phone's normal home screen at any time from settings

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no ads and no analytics
• The app has no internet permission, so your family's names, numbers and photos never leave the phone
• Direct calling is optional. It is used only if the family turns on one-tap call; otherwise the phone's dialer opens with the number filled in.

Notes
• To make it the home screen, the app asks you to choose Ek Chhoan as the default Home app. You can undo this at any time in settings.
• The YouTube, Camera and Gallery buttons open the apps already installed on your phone. Ek Chhoan is an independent app and is not made by or affiliated with Google or YouTube.

Questions or suggestions: imranansari321@pntapps.com
```

```
एक छुअन (Ek Chhoan) बुज़ुर्गों के लिए एक आसान होम स्क्रीन है। यह भरी-भरी फ़ोन स्क्रीन की जगह एक शांत पेज दिखाता है, जिसे बिना पढ़े या टाइप किए इस्तेमाल किया जा सकता है।
```
